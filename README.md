# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Using SQL

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn data for a telecommunications company using **MySQL**. The objective is to identify factors influencing customer churn, evaluate revenue performance, and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and business profitability.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and long-term business growth.

The company wants to understand:

* 🔍 Why customers leave
* ⚠️ Which customers are at the highest risk
* 💰 Which customer segments generate the most revenue
* 📈 What strategies can reduce churn

# 📊 Key Findings

## 👥 Customer Overview

* 👥 Total Customers: **7,043**
* ✅ Active Customers: **5,174**
* ❌ Churned Customers: **1,869**
* 📉 Overall Churn Rate: **26.54%**

---

## ⚠️ Major Churn Drivers

* 📅 Month-to-Month Contracts (**42.71%**)
* 💳 Electronic Check Payments (**45.29%**)
* 🌐 Fiber Optic Customers (**41.89%**)
* ⏳ First-Year Customers (**47.44%**)
* 👴 Senior Citizens (**41.68%**)

---

## 💰 Revenue Insights

* 💵 Total Revenue: **16.06 Million**
* 🏆 Two-Year Contracts generated the highest customer value.
* 🌐 Fiber Optic customers generated the highest revenue.
* 📈 Active customers generated significantly higher lifetime revenue than churned customers.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* 🎯 Encourage customers to migrate to long-term contracts.
* 🤝 Improve first-year customer onboarding and engagement.
* 🔍 Investigate the high churn rate among Fiber Optic customers.
* 🛡️ Promote Online Security, Tech Support, Online Backup, and Device Protection.
* 💳 Encourage automatic payment methods.
* 🌟 Implement loyalty programs for high-value customers.
